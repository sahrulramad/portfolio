Projects.

UMKM Success Prediction

Data Science Competition, DSC INFEST26 · 2026 · Python, scikit-learn, Pandas, Matplotlib

github.com/sahrulramad/dsc-infest26-umkm

Built a binary classification model that predicts whether a small business (UMKM) will succeed, from 12 business-profile attributes. Compared five algorithms under Stratified 5-Fold cross-validation on an imbalanced dataset where only about 25% of businesses are labelled successful.

The winning setup was a class-weighted Logistic Regression with an F1-optimized decision threshold derived from out-of-fold predictions. Permutation feature importance became four concrete policy recommendations for incubators and local government: internet adoption, business planning, capital access, and financial recordkeeping. The model is positioned as a screening tool, not an oracle.

Results

Algorithms compared5 under Stratified 5-Fold CV
Dataset250 profiles, 12 attributes
Positive class~25% (imbalanced)
Outcome4 policy recommendations

Dominant-Intelligence Classification & Major-Recommendation DSS

Undergraduate thesis · 2026 · Python, scikit-learn, Flask

Random Forest classifier that identifies a student's dominant intelligence (IQ / EQ / AQ / SQ), paired with a transparent AHP-SAW decision-support system (Analytic Hierarchy Process + Simple Additive Weighting) that ranks 19 university majors using expert-defined weights. The full pipeline is deployed as a Flask web app a school counselor can sit a student in front of.

97% accuracy, 5-fold CV, 200+ records
